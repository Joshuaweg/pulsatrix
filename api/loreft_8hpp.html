Detailed Description

LoReFT (Wu et al., 2024): fine-tune a frozen language model by editing its hidden states in a learned low-rank subspace, at a few positions after chosen layers, and explain the edit with LRP.

At each chosen position, the residual stream h after a block becomes 

h' = h + R^T (W h + b - R h)

with R (r, d) orthonormal rows. Inside the r-dimensional subspace R spans, the edit replaces the hidden state's coordinates R h by the learned W h + b; outside it, h is untouched. The model's own weights stay frozen. The edit is affine in h, so the epsilon rule propagates relevance through it exactly, and LoReFTIntervention splits the relevance into the part the edit carries and the part that passes through untouched.
