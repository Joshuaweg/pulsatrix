Detailed Description

Frosst and Hinton's soft decision tree, (N, inputs) to (N, classes) log-probabilities.

Note
The routing and the leaves are computed on the host in double precision; the gates' linear layer runs on the backend's device, and the output comes back on the input's device.
LRP isn't defined for it: propagate_relevance() throws. Explain it with its paths, or with gradient explainers.
