double pulsatrix::RipsOptions::threshold = -1

The largest scale; simplices with a larger diameter are left out. A negative value (the default) uses the enclosing radius, min over points of the largest distance from that point. At that scale the complex is a cone, which has no loops or voids, so no feature in dimension 1 or above is cut short.
