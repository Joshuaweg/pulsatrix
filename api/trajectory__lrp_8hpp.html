Detailed Description

Explaining a continuous diffusion sampler end to end (DIFF-5): trajectory LRP (design D1), which seeds a scalar of the final sample x_0 once and carries its relevance back through every reverse step to the starting noise x_T, the conditioning, and named sinks.

A DDPM or DDIM step is x_prev = skip * x_t - network * eps_theta(x_t, t, c) + noise * z, a residual block with a third, injected-noise term. The relevance on each element of x_prev is split across the three terms, by default keeping each term's sign (TrajectorySplit::SignedRatio). The skip share goes straight to x_t; the network share runs through eps_theta with its own LRP rules onto x_t, the conditioning c and the timestep embedding (a sink); the noise share is a sink too, exactly zero under DDIM. Only the trajectory is stored, and each step's forward pass is rerun just before its relevance pass. See docs/interpretability/diffusion-lrp.md (D1) for the derivation and its limits.
