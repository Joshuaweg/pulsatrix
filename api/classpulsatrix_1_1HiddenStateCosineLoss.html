Detailed Description

DistilBERT's cosine loss: the mean over kept rows of 1 − cos(s, t), with s and t the student's and teacher's vectors, (..., width); PyTorch's CosineEmbeddingLoss with target 1.

Note
Computed on the host in double precision; the gradient comes back on the student's device. A row whose student or teacher vector is zero counts as cos = 0, with no gradient.
