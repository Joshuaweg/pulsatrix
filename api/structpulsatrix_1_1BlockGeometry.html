Detailed Description

Each block's code geometry over x, from the covariance of its codes on the inputs where it is active: stable rank ‖C‖²_F / ‖C‖²_2, participation ratio (tr C)² / ‖C‖²_F and effective rank (the exponential of the eigenvalues' entropy). The paper finds a block's dimension saturates at the concept's (2 to 4 for DINOv3) however large b is.
