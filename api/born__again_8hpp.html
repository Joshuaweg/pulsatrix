Detailed Description

Born-again networks (Furlanello et al., ICML 2018; roadmap KD-4): a student the size of its teacher, trained on the teacher's outputs, then a teacher for the next generation.

Train a model on the labels; train a second with the same architecture on the first's softened outputs (DistillationLoss); repeat. Furlanello et al. found the students often beat their teachers, and an ensemble of the generations did better still. Later work found the gains small and not always there, so measure them over several seeds before believing them.

Two pieces here; the training loop is yours:

  • EnsembleLogProbabilities: the generations' mean distribution, as log-probabilities. It is the born-again ensemble's prediction, and, passed to DistillationLoss as the teacher's logits (softmax(log p) = p), a teacher made of several generations.
  • PermuteDarkKnowledge: Furlanello et al.'s control (DKPP). Each row keeps its largest logit where it is and shuffles the others among the other classes. The teacher's top class and its confidence stay; what it says about the other classes, the dark knowledge, is scrambled. If a student taught this way does as well, the gain didn't come from the dark knowledge.

Not here: their other control, CWTM, which weights each example's label loss by the teacher's confidence. It needs per-example weights in the cross-entropy loss.
