Detailed Description

Representation Topology Divergence (RTD): how differently two representations of the same inputs are shaped, cluster by cluster and loop by loop, and CompareRepresentations(), one report with CKA, mutual-kNN alignment and RTD side by side.

CKA compares two representations' whole geometry at once, and can score two layers as similar when one has clusters or loops the other lacks. RTD (Barannikov, Trofimov, Balabin and Burnaev, ICML 2022) compares their topology at every scale. Build the Vietoris-Rips filtration of a graph on two copies of the inputs, whose distances mix both representations; its barcode, the R-Cross-Barcode, records the features one representation has at a scale where the other has already lost them. RTD is the total length of its H1 bars. It is 0 for identical representations, and grows with every cluster that merges at a different scale.
