Detailed Description

Turning a persistence diagram into numbers for probes, statistics and plots: Betti curves, persistence landscapes, persistence images and persistent entropy.

A diagram is a multiset of points, which a classifier or a t-test can't take directly. These map it to fixed-size vectors. They differ in what they keep and in how stable they are:

Vectorization Stable under Certified here
Persistence landscape the bottleneck distance, 1-Lipschitz in the sup norm (Bubenik 2015) yes
Persistence image the 1-Wasserstein distance (Adams et al. 2017) no
Betti curve nothing useful: one point crossing a grid value changes it by 1 no
Persistent entropy not in general no

Essential features (infinite death) are left out of every one of them except the Betti curve, where they count as alive from their birth on. Report how many there were alongside.
