Detailed Description

Three views of a persistence diagram document (TDA-9): the persistence diagram, the barcode and the Betti curve.

The figures follow svg.hpp's rules: standalone files, deterministic bytes, no font library. Each dimension has its own color. Every point and bar carries a tooltip (<title>) with its birth, death and, when tested, p-value.
