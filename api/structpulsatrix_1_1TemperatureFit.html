bool pulsatrix::TemperatureFit::converged = true

False when the minimum is at a bound of T in [1e-6, 1e6]: the loss keeps falling as T grows (the logits are no better than chance, or point the wrong way) or as T shrinks (the logits separate the labels perfectly). temperature is then that bound.
