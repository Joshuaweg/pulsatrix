Detailed Description

Nearest neighbors over embeddings or activations, mutual nearest neighbors, and mutual-kNN alignment between two representations.

The questions these answer: which inputs does a layer treat as alike, which items of two sets pair up, and do two models agree about which inputs are alike (Huh et al., "The Platonic Representation Hypothesis", ICML 2024)? Everything is exact (no approximate index) and computed in double precision on the host, at about n × m × width multiply-adds for n queries and m points.

In high dimensions a few points can turn up in almost everyone's neighbor list ("hubs"), which makes neighbor-based scores mean less. MeasureHubness() says how much that is happening.
