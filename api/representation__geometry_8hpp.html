Detailed Description

One number per layer for the shape of its representations: anisotropy, effective rank and TwoNN intrinsic dimension.

Language models' representations are anisotropic (Ethayarajh, EMNLP 2019): they share a few large common directions, so the cosine similarity of two unrelated inputs is high, often 0.5 or more, and raw cosine similarity says little about meaning. Anisotropy() measures that, and shows how much centering removes. Effective rank says how many directions a layer spreads its inputs along, and TwoNN how many dimensions the inputs actually vary in locally, which can be far fewer than the width.

Everything is computed in double precision on the host.
