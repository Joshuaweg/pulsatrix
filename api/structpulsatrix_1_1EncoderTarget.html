MaskedToken 

The logit of token at residue, with that residue masked.

Mutation 

logit(token) - logit(wild_type) at residue, masked: a mutation's log-odds, the masked-marginal score (VariantScorer) before log-softmax, which cancels.

ProteinHead 

A per-protein head on the mean of the residues' representations (<cls> and <eos> left out, as ESM embeddings are usually pooled), unmasked.

ResidueHead 

A per-residue head on residue's representation, unmasked.
