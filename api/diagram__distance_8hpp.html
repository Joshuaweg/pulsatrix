Detailed Description

Distances between persistence diagrams, bottleneck and Wasserstein, and Certified<T>, a value with the error bounds behind it.

Two diagrams are compared by matching their points: each point either to a point of the other diagram or to the diagonal (where a feature of zero persistence would sit). The bottleneck distance is the smallest possible largest move; the q-Wasserstein distance the smallest q-th root of the sum of q-th powers of the moves. Moves are measured in the L-infinity norm, as in the stability theorem, so a point (b, d) is (d - b) / 2 from the diagonal.

The bottleneck distance is the one stability is stated in: two point clouds whose distance matrices differ by at most e have diagrams within e (Chazal, de Silva and Oudot 2014). It lets layers, models and seeds be compared with an error bar, which is why it returns a Certified value.
