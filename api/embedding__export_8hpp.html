Detailed Description

Hand embeddings or activations to UMAP and t-SNE tools, and bring their coordinates back as a projection document.

pulsatrix doesn't implement UMAP or t-SNE. Both are expensive, have many settings that change the picture, and are well served elsewhere. Both also distort the data: they keep each point's nearest neighbors, but distances between clusters, cluster sizes and densities in their plots carry little meaning, and an elongated or empty region can come from the settings alone. Use PCA (pca.hpp) for a view whose distances mean something, and these for a view of the neighborhoods.

This header writes the data in the formats those tools read:

  • **.npy** (SerializeNpy(), WriteNpy()): numpy's own format, which umap-learn, openTSNE and scikit-learn's TSNE take after np.load.
  • TSV for the Embedding Projector (ProjectorVectorsTsv(), ProjectorMetadataTsv()): projector.tensorflow.org runs UMAP, t-SNE and PCA in the browser on uploaded files, with nothing to install.

ExportEmbeddings() writes all of them to a directory. ParseNpy() and ReadNpy() read the coordinates the tools return, and MakeProjectionDocument() in viz/document.hpp turns them into a pulsatrix.projection.v1 document for the SVG, HTML and notebook views, which mark UMAP and t-SNE plots as distorting distances.
