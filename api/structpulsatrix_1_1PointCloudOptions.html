int64_t pulsatrix::PointCloudOptions::feature_axis = -1

The axis of an added tensor that holds the features, for every pooling but Flatten. Every other axis after the batch is a position. -1, the last axis, suits a sequence model's (batch, tokens, width); 1 suits a convolution's (batch, channels, height, width), whose positions are then its pixels in row-major order.
