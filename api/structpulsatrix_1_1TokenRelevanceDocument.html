Detailed Description

pulsatrix.token_relevance.v1: relevance scores over the pieces of a text, in reading order. MakeTokenRelevanceDocument() and MakeWordRelevanceDocument() (viz/text_relevance.hpp) build one from a tokenizer's Encoding or from word scores.
