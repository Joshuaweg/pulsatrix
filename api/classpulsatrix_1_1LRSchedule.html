Detailed Description

A learning-rate multiplier as a function of the step, with the formulas of Hugging Face transformers' get_{constant,linear,cosine}_schedule_with_warmup.

Note
Warmup ramps linearly from 0 at step 0 to 1 at step warmup. Linear then decays to 0 at step total; cosine follows half a cosine down to min_ratio at total. Both stay at their final value past total.
