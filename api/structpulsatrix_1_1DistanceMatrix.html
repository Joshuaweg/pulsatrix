Detailed Description

The distances between every pair of n points, condensed.

Pairs are stored as scipy's pdist stores them, (0, 1), (0, 2), ..., (0, n-1), (1, 2), ..., so a matrix can go to or come from Python unchanged, as RSA's Rdm does.

◆ error_bound

double pulsatrix::DistanceMatrix::error_bound = 0

A bound on the error of every stored distance: |values[k] - exact| <= error_bound for every pair. "Exact" means the distance between the points as given, or, with center or normalize, as centered and normalized in exact arithmetic.

Note
It is rigorous, not an estimate, under the standard model of floating-point arithmetic with float32 accumulation in the GEMM (no TF32 or other reduced-precision modes). It covers the dot products, the double-precision arithmetic, the rounding of centered or normalized points to float32, and the final rounding to float32.
